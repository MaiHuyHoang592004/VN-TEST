Marketing value-prop card — the "Everything you need" row on a landing page.

```jsx
<div style={{display:"grid",gridTemplateColumns:"repeat(4,1fr)",gap:"var(--gap-card)"}}>
  <FeatureCard icon={<Package size={23} />} title="Wide product range">
    Hundreds of customizable products across multiple categories.
  </FeatureCard>
</div>
```

Rules: 3 or 4 per row, never 5+. Copy is two sentences maximum. All cards in a row share one `tone` — a rainbow row is not GWP.
