Prefer

·Large intentional colour fields
·Asymmetric but balanced — a wide focal block beside a narrow rail
·Generous, uneven negative space
·Occasional overlap between branded and operational regions
·Product imagery as part of the composition

Avoid

·Equal-sized cards from top to bottom
·One uniform grid for the whole page
·Every section wrapped in its own box
·A KPI-dashboard stack of small modules
·Density without hierarchy
