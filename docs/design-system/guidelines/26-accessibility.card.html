The exception, stated plainly. Cream display type on Bright Sky measures 1.5:1 and does not meet WCAG, even at the 3:1 large-text threshold. It is the approved brand direction for hero and large brand type, so it is permitted there only — never for labels, controls, body copy, data or anything a user must read to operate the product.
Always recoverable. Page identity is duplicated in accessible form: the active nav pill (navy on cream, 11.5:1) and the document title. Where a hero must carry cream type and pass, PageHero tone="deep" lifts it to 3.6:1 — AA at large sizes.
