The mark is brand light, not information ink. Light sky on cream, white and the floating nav shell; cream on bright sky. Navy is an information colour — a navy mark reads heavy and corporate and pulls the brand toward SaaS/admin, so it is never the default. Readability. The logo sky is a step stronger than the page sky so the mark separates from a cream ground while still feeling light.
