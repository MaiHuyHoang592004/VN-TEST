Page vs Drawer vs Modal vs Popover · the containment decision Drawer · Modal · Popover · CommandPalette

Default to overlaying in place — a full navigation should be reserved for surfaces that earn their own address. Ask in order: does it need a shareable URL, or is it wider than ~560px? → page. Editing or reading one record over a list? → drawer. A short self-contained task or confirm? → modal. A quick pick at the trigger? → popover.

1 · Keep the page
Product detail/products/882

WHEN → shareable URL, SEO, deep-link, or wider than ~560px.

Product / order detail, dashboards, list workbenches, marketing, auth. A 560px drawer can't hold a two-column matrix — that stays a page (e.g. the full price table).

3 · Modal — centred, self-contained
Kết nối shop

WHEN → a short task with no list to sit beside, or a confirm.

Connect shop, TTS import mapping, create key, image zoom, delete confirm. Centred sibling of Drawer. Don't put a browsing surface in a modal.

4 · Popover — anchored, quick pick
Orders Date range ▾

WHEN → a small choice made right at its trigger.

Date range, column chooser, row-action menu, mockup thumbnail. Portals to fixed coords so a parent's overflow:hidden can't clip it. For a ~30-route map, that's CommandPalette (⌘K), not a menu.
